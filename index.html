THE SHIFT

where convenience beats fear.

Signal

People want a personal AI — but fear for their privacy, autonomous agents and losing control.

Tension

To make an intelligence smart and truly useful, you have to feed it more of your private life. Which means opening up.

Story

Alex, a startup founder after a string of failures, creates EXTRA — a personal intelligence meant to help him build a great product on his own. For EXTRA to become truly useful, Alex has to share his private life with it. He starts to see himself from the outside — and to change.

Point of view

The audience discovers the world through EXTRA’s “eyes and ears” — it sees and hears the physical world through wearable AI devices.
