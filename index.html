03 · Method

We find the audience first. Then we build the world.

How the work runs inside every test.

01 · SIGNALS

Read the conversation

Thousands of discussions on Reddit, TikTok, YouTube and forums. Not trends — the fears, desires and arguments that don’t go away.

02 · STORY

Build the world

Turn the tension into a central question. Design world rules that force hard character choices.

03 · FAST PRODUCTION

Move while it’s hot

Audience interest is short-lived. AI trailers and a micro-series go live to measure response. An idea with no response is reworked or stopped.

04 · GAME MECHANICS

The audience plays

Viewers don’t watch ads — they take part: decode artifacts, pick a side of the conflict, unlock the world step by step.

05 · UGC

Creators amplify

We give formats, templates and keys to the world. Remixes and theories spread without ads.

06 · CINEMA

Fans become ticket buyers

By premiere day the audience is built, the conversation is live, the communities are ready. Reactions fuel the next chapter.

05 · FAQ

Common objections.

“Social buzz doesn’t predict box office.”

Agreed — research shows it adds little once the budget is known. That’s why we measure actions, not views: sign-ups and “notify me at premiere”.

“Who are you in the industry?”

25 years in TV production (3 TV channels, 40M+ households) and 10 years building technology products. We bring product-validation methods from tech into film development.

“What do you need from me now?”

Nothing up front. Until production, we fund everything ourselves. We come to you with the evidence pack.

“Do you have skin in the game?”

Yes. At production, the author invests 10% of the budget — which is why we test everything carefully.

“What if the test fails?”

Then the project is reworked or stopped before production money is spent. That is the point of the test.

“Who owns the rights?”

THE SHIFT assets are held by Inner Inc. (US). Chain of title available under NDA.
